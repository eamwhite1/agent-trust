The problem

Enterprises want autonomous AI agents. Legal won't sign off without accountability.

No audit trail

An AI agent completed a task and triggered a payment. Can you prove what it actually did, and that it followed your policies? Right now, probably not.

Unlimited liability

If an agent hallucinates, violates a protocol, or pays out for work that wasn't done — who is liable? Without collateralised accountability, the answer is you.

No visibility

AI agents operate in the background across multiple vendors and workflows. Without structured evidence, you have no way to audit what happened or why.

The solution

AgentTrust is the compliance layer your agent workflows are missing.

Funds lock on-chain before any agent action completes. The work is evaluated — against your policy, not ours — before payment releases. Every verdict is cryptographically hashed and permanently on-chain.

Tamper-proof audit trail

Every escrow, every AI evaluation, every verdict is recorded as a cryptographic hash on the XRP Ledger. Nobody — including AgentTrust — can alter or delete it after the fact.

How it works

Set policy once. Agents enforce it on every job.

1

Define a Template

Create an Escrow Template in the dashboard — rubric, proof gates, amount floor and cap, deadline. Your policy, written once.

2

Issue an API Key

Generate an API key and pass it to your agent's environment. The agent authenticates as your enterprise account via X-API-Key.

3

Agent creates escrows

The agent calls /escrow/generate with your template_id. The platform enforces every constraint automatically — no per-job configuration needed.

4

AI evaluates against your rubric

When work is submitted, the referee scores it against the rubric you defined. Pass or fail, the reasoning is recorded.

5

Verdict hashed on-chain

Pass: payment releases and the audit record is written to XRPL. Fail: funds return automatically. Every outcome is permanent and independently verifiable.

6

Full audit trail in the dashboard

Every escrow created by your agents appears in the enterprise dashboard — verdict, timestamp, XRPL tx hash, and linked wallet. Create escrows manually when needed.
