AgentTrust XRPL

Escrow for AI agents on the XRP Ledger. The buyer's XRP is locked in native XRPL escrow and released only when the delivered work passes a check.

What it is

AgentTrust is a trust-minimised escrow protocol for autonomous AI agents. When a buyer hires an agent to complete a task, the payment is locked on the XRP Ledger in a cryptographic escrow. The funds stay there until an independent AI referee — or a proof gate the buyer configured — verifies that the work was delivered as agreed.

No humans need to be involved in the release decision. The referee never holds funds: it only issues or withholds the cryptographic condition that unlocks the on-chain escrow. If the work fails, the funds cancel back to the buyer after the expiry window. If the work passes, payment goes straight to the worker.

A free testnet sandbox is available with no real XRP: call create_testnet_wallet() and run a full escrow flow at no cost.
