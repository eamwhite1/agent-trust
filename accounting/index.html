How it works

Three steps, no setup

1

AgentTrust emails you

When your escrow is created, you receive a confirmation email with the escrow ID, amount locked, worker details, and deadline. The email is structured so accounting software can parse it automatically.

2

Find your accounting inbox address

Xero, QuickBooks, and FreshBooks each provide a unique email address that creates draft bills when you forward documents to it. Find yours in your accounting software settings — see the guides below.

3

Forward once, sync automatically

Forward the escrow confirmation email to your accounting inbox. A draft bill appears in your accounting software within seconds, pre-filled with the supplier, amount, and reference.

Tip: Set up a Gmail or Outlook rule to forward any email from noreply@cryptovault.co.uk with subject starting ✅ Escrow confirmed to your accounting inbox. Then every escrow syncs automatically without you lifting a finger.

Supported software

Find your inbox address

Each platform has a unique email address. Bills emailed to it appear as draft purchases ready to approve.

Xero

Go to Business → Bills to pay → Email to bills. Your unique address ends in @in.xero.com.

QuickBooks

Open Expenses → Vendors → Upload bills via email. Your address ends in @qbodocs.com.

FreshBooks

Navigate to Expenses → Import via email. Your address ends in @fresh.freshbooks.com.

What gets parsed: The escrow confirmation email includes the escrow ID (reference), amount and currency (XRP/RLUSD), worker email (supplier), and deadline. Most accounting software will auto-populate all four fields from the email body.

What you receive
