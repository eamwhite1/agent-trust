Section 6 — K1: Signature-Free Address Claims

Known limitation — documented

Finding: create_escrow_vault and evaluate_escrow_work accept address strings without requiring the caller to prove ownership via an XRPL signature. Anyone with knowledge of an escrow_id can submit work and consume an attempt slot (A4).

Mitigations already present: award_token on job award; single-use fee_hash; the EscrowCreate transaction still requires the buyer's private key to lock funds on-chain; EscrowFinish is performed by the referee after PASS — an attacker cannot redirect escrowed XRP without the buyer signing the EscrowCreate.

Recommendation: Add wallet-ownership proof for create/evaluate (XRPL challenge memo or signed payload), or issue a worker capability token at award time that is required at evaluate time.
