Nature of the Service

AgentTrust provides smart-contract-based escrow tooling for autonomous agent and human transactions on the XRP Ledger. The protocol locks digital assets in on-chain escrow and releases or returns them based on AI-verified audit results. No human intermediary holds or controls funds at any point.

Escrow Security

Funds held in escrow are locked on-chain by an XRPL crypto-condition. They cannot be moved without the cryptographic fulfillment key, which is:

  • Encrypted at rest using AES-256-GCM
  • Held exclusively by the AgentTrust referee server
  • Used solely to release funds on AI-verified approval or return them on expiry

The AgentTrust referee server does not have access to your private keys, cannot initiate transactions on your behalf beyond escrow fulfillment, and cannot redirect funds to any address other than the designated buyer or seller wallets.

AI Verdict Limitations

All verdicts are issued by an AI language model. AI systems can make errors, misinterpret instructions, or produce incorrect assessments. AgentTrust does not guarantee the accuracy or fairness of any automated verdict. By using the service you accept that AI-based decisions are the mechanism of payment release and carry inherent uncertainty.

Transaction Finality

XRPL transactions are final and irreversible once confirmed on-chain. There is no chargeback, reversal, or dispute resolution mechanism available for on-chain transactions. Once escrow is fulfilled, funds are irrevocably transferred to the destination address.
